**ADS: Apocalypse Delivery Service** is a Death Stranding-inspired solo journaling TTRPG about delivering packages.

The idea behind this game is that you are making deliveries across a devastated world, connecting the last pieces of humanity. As you complete deliveries, you write a journal of the things you see and the deliveries you make, as well as reflecting emotionally on what they mean to you and to the people you're connecting. You make a certain number of deliveries, ending with a final "special" delivery, to meet the quota for your shift. There aren't very many actual mechanics involved, with the focus being on creative journaling. It's also currently a relatively short game - probably 30 minutes to an hour, depending on how in-depth you write.

For this project, I tried to limit myself to making a one-pager (mostly because I wasn't sure how much I'd be able to write). I had a few ideas for mechanics that I didn't get to put in as well, which are in the brainstorming doc. I'd potentially be interested in expanding this one in the future, both in terms of amount of mechanics and amount of writing.
